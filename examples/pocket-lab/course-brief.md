# Pocket Lab / version 1.0.0

Status: completed artifact demonstration; not child-piloted. This is an original sample, not a reconstruction of an unavailable prior course.

## Assumptions chosen for this demonstration

- One beginner around 11, comfortable with a sustained supported reading and whole-number addition.
- One week, two 45-minute sessions (90 minutes core). Household paper, ruler, pencil, tape, scissors; no purchase budget or currency assumed.
- Adult launches sessions, checks measurements, supports reading/cutting as needed. About 10 minutes initial preparation and 3 minutes before session 2.
- English, centimetres, US Letter, black-and-white-friendly printing. No child name, gender, diagnosis, or account required.
- Selected domains: math, reading, writing, critical thinking, creative thinking.
- Selected formats: workbook, complete long reading, physical interactive design/testing, printable PDF materials, separate adult guide. Digital interaction not selected.

Capstone: paper pocket prototype plus sketches, calculations, tests, and explanatory label. Starting/ending check compares how specifically the learner describes a useful pocket and its evidence.

## Session map

- W01-S01: launch 5 + reading 12 + responses 10 + measurement 13 + review/cleanup 5 = 45 minutes. Student pp. 1–5.
- W01-S02: recall 3 + design 8 + build 12 + test 7 + revise 8 + label/reflection 5 + cleanup 2 = 45 minutes. Student pp. 6–8.
- Optional challenges extend core time. Each adult session note supplies a 20-minute route with explicit deferrals.
