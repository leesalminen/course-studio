## Design Context

### Users
Parents of children aged 9–15 creating personalized homeschool or enrichment courses. The landing page should explain the offering, show a complete example, and make starting a course easy. Confirmed by the creator on September 15, 2026.

### Brand Personality
Warm, calm, creative. Encourage curiosity and give parents confidence without hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
A simple editorial page with warm paper tones, deep green typography, a restrained terracotta accent, and an illustrated course booklet. Distinctive serif headings paired with clear sans-serif text. Light theme, generous breathing room, minimal motion.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the child's curiosity and the family's practical needs.
- Make the setup message easy to find, copy, and use.
- Show a real example and describe its limitations honestly.
- Use typography and spacing for hierarchy; keep containers and decoration purposeful.
- Keep the page lightweight, keyboard accessible, and comfortable on small screens.
